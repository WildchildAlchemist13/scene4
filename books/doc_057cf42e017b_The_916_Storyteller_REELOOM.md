The HBC Architecture

EE

Every episode of every successful micro-drama on every platform follows the same
three-part structure. I call it the HBC Architecture: Hook, Beat, Cliffhanger.
Miss any single element — even once — and viewers do not forgive you. They swipe.

attack.

What an Archetype Actually Is
An archetype is not a cliché. A cliché is a character the audience has seen so many
times they feel nothing. An archetype is a character type the audience recognises

R

immediately — but has never seen this way.
The Billionaire. The Underdog. The Hidden Heir. The Ice Queen. The Wicked Rival.
Every one of these has been done a thousand times. Every one of them can still
electrify an audience — if you give them a specific, unexpected layer underneath the
recognisable surface.

The formula: Familiar exterior. Surprising interior.

What Does Not Work (Stop Writing These)

Wide establishing shots

Fifty wedding guests reacting to a revelation. A packed boardroom witnessing a
